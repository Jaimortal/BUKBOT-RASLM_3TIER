[TITLE] About ICT Services

[KEYWORDS]
ICT, ICT-SU, ICT services, information technology, computer labs, technical support

[ANSWER - EN]
The Information & Communications Technology Services Unit (ICT-SU) of Bukidnon State University handles the technical aspects of university processes including information systems, network services, technical services, and web development. ICT-SU is responsible for maintaining computers in the computer laboratories and ensuring that any issues or damages are addressed promptly. Students may approach ICT-SU for technical assistance regarding computer facilities.

[ANSWER - CEB]
Ang Information & Communications Technology Services Unit (ICT-SU) sa Bukidnon State University mao ang ga-atiman sa teknikal nga bahin sa mga proseso sa unibersidad lakip ang information systems, network, technical services, ug web development. Sila usab ang ga-mentinar sa mga computer sulod sa computer laboratory ug naga-ayo sa mga problema niini. Pwede ka mo-doul sa ila alang sa technical nga tabang.

[TITLE] How to Get WiFi Access

[KEYWORDS]
wifi, internet access, ICT wifi, connect wifi, ICT office location, wifi access

[ANSWER - EN]
Here is how to get WiFi access from ICT:
1. Go to the Finance Building, 3rd floor.
2. Locate the Audio Laboratory.
3. In front of it, you will find the ICT office.
4. Look for Computer Laboratory 6 (ComLab 6).
5. The ICT office is located on the right side of ComLab 6.

[ANSWER - CEB]
Ani ang paagi aron makakuha og WiFi:
1. Adto sa Finance Building, ikatulong andana.
2. Pangitaa ang Audio Laboratory.
3. Atubangan niini makita ang ICT office.
4. Pangitaa ang Computer Laboratory 6 (ComLab 6).
5. Ang ICT office naa sa tuo nga bahin sa ComLab 6.

ICT mission, purpose of ICT, ICT goal

[ANSWER - EN]
